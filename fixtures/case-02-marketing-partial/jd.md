# Digital Marketing Manager

## About the role

We are looking for a Digital Marketing Manager to own the top of our
funnel: SEO, content strategy, and paid acquisition. You will work
directly with the founders and be measured on qualified pipeline.

## Requirements

- 3+ years in digital marketing, ideally at a B2B SaaS company
- Hands-on SEO experience: keyword research, on-page optimisation, and
  technical audits
- Google Analytics 4 for reporting and dashboarding
- Content strategy: editorial calendars, briefs, and working with
  freelance writers
- Paid acquisition on Google Ads and LinkedIn Ads
- A/B testing on landing pages, with a clear methodology for calling
  a winner
- HubSpot for campaign management and lead scoring
- Comfort presenting funnel metrics to the executive team

## Nice to have

- Experience in a Series A or Series B startup
- Basic SQL for pulling ad-hoc marketing data
- HTML and CSS for editing landing pages directly

## What we offer

Full ownership of the marketing function, direct access to the founders,
and a budget you help shape.
