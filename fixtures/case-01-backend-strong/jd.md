# Senior Backend Engineer

## About the role

We are hiring a Senior Backend Engineer to own the services behind our
payments platform. The team runs on Python and FastAPI, deploys through
Docker to AWS, and stores everything in PostgreSQL.

## Requirements

- 5+ years of professional backend development
- Strong Python and FastAPI experience
- PostgreSQL, including schema design and query tuning
- Docker for local development and production containers
- AWS in production: at least ECS or EKS, plus S3 and RDS
- Comfort with REST API design and versioning
- Experience owning a service in production, including on-call

## Nice to have

- Kafka or another event stream
- OpenTelemetry or a comparable observability stack
- Terraform

## What we offer

Small team, direct ownership of the payments domain, and a codebase
that values clarity over cleverness.
