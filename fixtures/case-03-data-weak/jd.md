# Data Scientist

## About the role

Our data science team builds and ships machine learning models into the
recommendation and fraud products. We are hiring a Data Scientist to own
one model end to end, from framing through production monitoring.

## Requirements

- 4+ years working as a Data Scientist or applied ML engineer
- Strong Python, including pandas, scikit-learn, and PyTorch
- Experience shipping at least one ML model to production, not just to
  a notebook
- Solid grounding in statistical modelling and experiment design
- SQL against a warehouse (BigQuery, Snowflake, or Redshift)
- Comfort with production monitoring: feature drift, model decay, and
  offline vs online metric gaps

## Nice to have

- MLOps tooling: MLflow, Weights and Biases, or SageMaker
- Prior work on recommendation systems or fraud

## What we offer

Direct ownership of a model in production, a team that treats data
science as engineering, and a company that ships weekly.
